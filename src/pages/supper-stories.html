Supper Stories

A supper club inspired by my book, A Table Full of Stories.

Supper Stories is an intimate, home-hosted gathering where family and friends come together around recipes from the book. Each session explores the ideas behind the book: traditional Indian recipes, seasonal ingredients, and simple changes that make everyday cooking healthier without compromising on flavour.

I present recipes from the book, share the stories and nutritional thinking behind them, and open the table up for conversation. Guests leave with recipe handouts so they can recreate the dishes in their own kitchens.

I believe small steps can make a real difference, and that change can begin right at our own doorstep. That's the heart of Supper Stories:

eat it here, cook it at home.

3 May 2026

Aam Panna

For a small group of guests, I presented the aam panna recipe from the book and explained its nutritional benefits. Raw mango is rich in vitamin C, roasted cumin aids digestion, black salt helps replace minerals lost through sweating, and mint has a natural cooling effect. We compared homemade panna with packaged coolers, which are often high in sugar and preservatives, and discussed using jaggery as a healthier sweetener. The session ended with everyone tasting the panna together.

12 July 2026

Jaggery Bites

I presented the jaggery bites recipe from the book, a traditional Indian sweet made with jaggery and dry fruits, and explained why it makes a healthier alternative to refined-sugar desserts. Jaggery is less processed than white sugar and retains minerals like iron and magnesium. Almonds, cashews and walnuts add protein and healthy fats, while dates and raisins provide fibre and natural sweetness. We talked about portion size, why these bites work well as an energy-boosting snack, and how easily the recipe can be adapted with whatever nuts and seeds are on hand. Guests took home the recipe card to try it themselves.
