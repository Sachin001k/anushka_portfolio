Work Experience

Ajanta Pharma Internship

I did my internship at Ajanta Pharmaceuticals, where I saw the entire process of how medicines are made — from formulation to production. It gave me a clear, practical understanding of how a drug actually comes together. The internship was recognized with the CREST Gold Award and the Global STEM Award.

BARC Internship

I did my internship at the Bhabha Atomic Research Centre (BARC), where I worked under a scientist studying cancer biology and immunology. I learnt about the underlying concepts and research methods in this area, and at the end of the internship, I wrote a report on therapeutic cancer vaccines.
